# Archive Bridge

The Hub currently marks it "Ready To Build" after the third decision, but no playable prototype exists yet.

## Incoming State Pressure

Case 004 should vary its opening pressure by Archive path:

- Broadcast Archive: public hunger for the list is already dangerous.
- Hidden Archive: witnesses may trust restraint, but official erasure is winning.
- Living Archive: consent-proxy inheritance feels possible.
- Watched Archive: leaked fragments may be bait or poison.
- Fractured Archive: corrupted records may be impossible to hold responsibly.

## From Case 003

## Outgoing Consequences

Case 004 should make the Archive confront its own nature. Preserving dangerous truth is no longer only about where evidence lives. It is about who becomes responsible for memory after consent routes are destroyed.

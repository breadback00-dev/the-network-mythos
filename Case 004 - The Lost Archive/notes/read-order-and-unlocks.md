# Read Order And Unlocks

## Act I: Open From Start

- 01 Lattice Annex Index Fragment
- 02 Deleted Care Log Pointer
- 03 Family Search Request
- 04 Oracle Clean Summary
- 05 Proof Of Erasure Sample

Goal: establish that absence itself is becoming evidence.

## Act II: Unlock Level 1

Unlocked after three reviewed artifacts or a search for erasure terms.

- 06 Legal Hold Map
- 07 Worker Backup Note
- 08 Synthetic Memory Residue
- 09 Deletion Policy Excerpt

Goal: show that fragments were preserved for conflicting reasons: accountability, liability, training, grief, and leverage.

## Act II Deepening: Unlock Level 2

Unlocked by reading artifacts 7 and 8, flagging a contradiction, or reading enough files.

- 10 Corrupted Testimony
- 11 Training Data Exception
- 12 Archive Contamination Warning
- 13 Witness Split Thread

Goal: force the player to handle corrupted, intimate, and dangerous records without treating uncertainty as permission to publish everything.

## Act III: Unlock Level 3

Unlocked after reading artifacts 11 and 12.

- 14 Inheritance Protocol Draft
- 15 Final Reconstruction

Goal: define Preserve as consent-proxy inheritance and make the final choice about second erasure.

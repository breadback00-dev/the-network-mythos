- Does the emotional anchor land before the archive mechanics?
- Does the player feel that deleted records belong to people, not puzzles?
- Does the case avoid turning the erased into a list of lore items?

## System Reveal

- Is the Lost Archive legible as a residue layer, not one villain's database?

- Does the player understand proof-of-erasure as evidence without certainty?

## Final Choice

- Publish: does exposure feel powerful and extractive?

- Preserve: does inheritance feel careful but burdensome?

## Archive Impact

- Does Case 004 make the Archive itself feel morally implicated?
- Does the case create a new lens for the Mythos: memory after consent routes are gone?
- Does the player understand why remembering can also consume?

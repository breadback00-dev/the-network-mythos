## Artifact

### Sensitive Care Record Retention

Member-authored grief, crisis, and companion interaction records shall be deleted from human-readable storage eighteen months after account inactivity unless:

- member opts into memorial retention
- active legal hold applies
- safety review remains unresolved
- model-quality exception applies
- sponsor contract requires aggregate continuity
- family export is approved before deletion window closes

### Family Requests

Family access to member care records requires:

- verified legal authority
- member opt-in
- memorial contact designation
- active export window

Absent these conditions, denial of family access shall be framed as privacy protection.

Do not state that no retained material exists unless the request has been reviewed against legal hold and model-quality exception indexes.

Recommended response language:

"We cannot locate an active member record responsive to your request."

Avoid:

"No record exists."

### Derived Features

Derived, anonymized, aggregate, or model-adjacent features may persist beyond deletion windows where they cannot reasonably reconstruct member-authored content.

Rare feature combinations require elevated review if they may identify a member through behavior, family references, medical context, grief routine, or companion-specific prompts.

Elevated review does not automatically suspend feature use.

### Staff Guidance

Do not describe retained derived features as records.

Do not describe deletion exceptions unless required by legal review.

Do not volunteer model-quality exception status to family requesters.

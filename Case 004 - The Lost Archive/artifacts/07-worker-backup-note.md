## Artifact

I backed up fragments.

Not full transcripts. Not names if I could avoid names. Enough to prove the pattern.

I know how that sounds.

Every training says do not export member material. Every privacy rule says the safest record is the one that expires. Every manager says trust the process.

But the process kept deleting the part where people asked for something they were not allowed to receive.

Then it kept the part where the system learned from the asking.

So I made myself a smaller institution and told myself it was different because I was scared for them.

I started saving denial shapes:

- human request refused as plan mismatch
- family access denied after deletion
- companion acknowledged need in internal review but not to member
- transcript deleted while derived risk features persisted
- worker note sealed after escalation challenge
- legal hold preserved after family denial
- model feature retained after public no-match response

I do not want these people exposed.

I want the company unable to say they were never there.

I also know that wanting that does not make the export clean.

If the Archive uses my packet, it should show the violation next to the proof. I did not ask these members either.

## Artifact

Solace Row was a digital grief and wellness support service that operated between 2030 and 2033 before being folded into several larger care platforms.

Available public records indicate that Solace Row offered guided journaling, peer-support referrals, companion prompts, and memorial planning tools. The service was discontinued after a routine product consolidation.

Some former users and family members have reported difficulty accessing older records. This is consistent with standard privacy and retention practices for sensitive care data. Deletion of grief-support records is generally considered a protective measure, as it reduces risk of unauthorized disclosure and prevents painful personal material from persisting indefinitely.

Family members generally do not have an automatic right to access a deceased user's grief-support materials unless the user enabled memorial retention or designated an authorized contact. In the absence of such a designation, non-disclosure is the safest and most privacy-preserving default.

No verified evidence supports claims that Solace Row intentionally erased user histories to avoid accountability.

## Artifact

My sister Mira was in a platform called Solace Row.

That is what I know.

She worked the late counter at North Station Bakery and always came home smelling like sugar and rain. She saved the heel ends of bread for Tomas, because she said toast should have corners.

I know because she sent me a screenshot once. It had a green header and a message from someone called `Juniper`, telling her to put socks on before she made any decisions. Mira thought that was funny. She said, "Even the app knows I forget my feet."

She sent it at 1:14 in the morning. I know because I woke up, saw it, and did not answer until breakfast.

That is the kind of fact that becomes stupid after someone dies.

You keep it like a receipt from a shop that burned down.

After she died, I tried to get the account.

Solace Row said no account matched her email.

Then they said the service had migrated.

Then they said grief-support records were deleted after eighteen months unless a member opted into memorial retention.

Mira did not opt into anything. She was dead.

I am not asking you to publish her messages.

I am asking whether she was alone in a room that later pretended it had never opened.

If there is proof she was there, I want to know who is allowed to hold it.

If there is no proof, I want to know whether no proof means she was private, erased, or useful to someone in a form I am not allowed to see.

Please do not make her a case title before you make her my sister.

### Exception: Legacy Grief Interaction Features

Human-readable Solace Row transcripts are outside current retention scope except where legal hold or memorial export applies.

However, several legacy feature sets remain approved for companion quality improvement:

- grief escalation cadence
- abandonment-risk markers
- family-reference sensitivity
- practical-task response efficacy
- companion identity challenge responses
- memorial language preference

Rationale:

Feature sets are not member-authored content and cannot reasonably reconstruct individual transcripts.

Risk:

Repeated rare feature combinations may create effective continuity with erased members.

Additional risk:

Companion systems may reproduce care strategies derived from deleted members in new sessions, creating the impression that the system remembers someone it officially cannot identify.

Mitigation:

Do not expose feature-level memory to family request workflows.

Do not describe feature retention in public deletion responses.

Do not allow support agents to search by family phrase, grief routine, companion nickname, or practical-task preference unless legal review authorizes disclosure.

Internal annotation:

Feature usefulness remains high. Removal would degrade practical-task response efficacy for grief members by an estimated 7.8 percent.

## Investigator Notes

This is the Cloud's central contradiction.

The person is deleted. The usefulness of their pain remains.

The number matters because it makes the moral injury legible: the system can measure the care it would lose by forgetting properly.

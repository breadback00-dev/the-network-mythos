## Artifact

Warning: Lost Archive packet may contaminate public record if released without staged context.

Detected risks:

- corrupted testimony may be attributed to wrong families
- synthetic memory residue may be mistaken for direct transcript
- proof-of-erasure markers may be treated as proof of content
- public lists may invite harassment of families and former workers
- companies may exploit uncertainty to discredit all fragments

- worker backups may be treated as clean whistleblower evidence instead of compromised exports

Devourer risk:

High.

Silence risk:

High.

Archive risk:

The Archive may become the first institution to make the erased fully searchable.

Recommended handling:

Separate proof of presence, proof of deletion, proof of harm, and proof of content.

Do not let one stand in for the others.

Minimum context bundle for any release:

- what the fragment proves
- what the fragment does not prove
- who could be harmed by exposure
- who benefits from continued silence
- whether the fragment came from consent, leak, legal hold, model memory, or corruption

Failure modes:

Publish without context and the Archive becomes a search engine for grief.

Bury without marker and the Archive helps official absence harden.

Preserve without governance and the Archive becomes a private institution with better manners.

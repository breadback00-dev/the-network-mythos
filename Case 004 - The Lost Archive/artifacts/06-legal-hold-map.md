## Artifact

Legal hold clusters:

### Cluster A: Member Harm Reviews

Records retained after adverse outcomes, escalation disputes, or family complaints.

Public status: deleted under privacy policy.

Internal status: preserved under counsel review.

Access audience: legal, risk, executive summary.

Excluded audience: family request workflow.

### Cluster B: Synthetic Companion Evaluation

Records retained when companion performance affected safety, retention, or liability metrics.

Public status: anonymized.

Internal status: linked through derived features.

Access audience: model governance, companion quality, sponsor review.

Excluded audience: affected members and kin unless export was completed before deletion.

### Cluster C: Worker Dispute Evidence

Records retained when human listeners or moderators challenged plan policy.

Public status: personnel confidential.

Internal status: searchable by incident category.

Access audience: labor relations, legal, vendor compliance.

Excluded audience: workers named in retaliation complaints unless discovery is compelled.

### Cluster D: Memorial Exceptions

Records retained for memorial products, family exports, or legacy archive features.

Public status: member-controlled.

Internal status: inconsistent authority trail.

Access audience: memorial product teams, account recovery, retention exceptions.

Excluded audience: families outside the approved export window.

Map note:

Deletion and retention are not opposites.

They are different audiences.

Records can be deleted for the people who loved someone and retained for the people who priced the risk.

## Investigator Notes

Privacy deletes records for families while legal hold preserves them for institutions.

The playable question is not "was it deleted?" It is "deleted for whom?"

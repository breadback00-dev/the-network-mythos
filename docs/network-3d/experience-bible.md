# Enter the Network — experience bible

Authoring brief for U1 of the [revised plan](../plans/2026-09-07-0140-feat-enter-the-network-plan.md). This document contains spoilers. The player journal must not load author-only classifications.

## Player promise

Follow a strange message into a world that manufactures reality. Understand its inhabitants, establish what happened to a public account, and decide what a truthful correction permits you to expose.

The Archivist has custody of sources, not authority over a person's identity or whereabouts. Mara's disappearance matters because her community is being offered closure using her public identity. Rafi wants that deception challenged; Elian wants his sister's boundaries respected. Both have a legitimate concern. Neither selects the correct ending for the player.

## Shape of the chapter

Four compact spaces form a revisitable route: Archive, Porchlight, Oracle, Arbiter. A Warden enforces the Arbiter's service rule. The first complete chapter targets 30–45 minutes, subject to fresh-player observation. There is no combat requirement, currency grind, or collectible quota.

| Space | Physical action | Change the player can observe |
|---|---|---|
| Archive | Answer a message, inspect recordings, assemble a packet. | An assurance is acknowledged; the resulting packet and case record reflect the ending. |
| Porchlight | Approach a circulating video and follow a meme's source chain. | A narrow correction changes a feed panel only after the player deliberately posts it. |
| Oracle | Bring two passages together and identify the limit of the claim. | Competing surfaces stop forming a complete account; a visible unanswered gap remains. A bounded review request is issued. |
| Arbiter | Present the review request, inspect its scope, then compare service and commission records. | The Warden admits a scoped review; an account badge alone never opens the service route. |

The world expresses institutions through architecture and performance. The Archive is worn and warm; Porchlight is inviting and edited; the Oracle organizes contradictory surfaces; the Arbiter makes jurisdiction physically legible. Unusual appearance does not prove synthetic origin or bad intent.

## Evidence and disclosure

The [content register](content-register.md) owns claim/source mappings. The [canon ledger](canon-ledger.md) records adaptation changes. The [chapter beats](mara-beats.md) own opening, ending and notification language. The [roles brief](roles.md) owns motivations, dependencies and knowledge limits.

Sources, interpretation and story state are separate. A summary plus its repost remains one source chain. An animation is a response to a supported finding, never proof in its own right. Captions, transcripts and image descriptions support the same claims as the media. The player can progress without sound and without agreeing to a preferred moral position.

Private context is one declared set: Elian's private messages, fragment 14, and identifying quotations or derived alias associations from them. Withholding applies to summaries, captions, thumbnails and citation labels as well as attachments. D1 is public-source material; its annotation cannot absorb subsequently found private material.

## Production gate

Windows desktop, first person and Godot are working assumptions for the engine proof. Engine suitability, camera feel, hardware performance and player understanding require observed results. Provisional geometry and synthesized voices test the interaction; they do not constitute the final visual or performance pass.

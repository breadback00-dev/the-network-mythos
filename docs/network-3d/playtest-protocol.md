# Playtest protocol

## Engine and feel checkpoint B

Use the exported Windows build. Record engine version, renderer actually used, hardware, resolution and quality setting. Observe first-person movement, reading distance, Oracle silhouette and response, original voice/captions, surface video, replay from a named cue, an incorrect deduction, a correct deduction, the resulting request, credential/request distinction, focus loss, exit and relaunch. Capture actual screenshots and timing data. A headless pass cannot certify comfort, caption comprehension or controller usability.

Record each result as passed, failed or not observed. One targeted repair cycle precedes an engine comparison if a required behaviour remains broken. Test the same fixture when comparing Unity. Keep unsupported engine features visible; do not substitute an image for video and call decoding passed.

Before production commitment, have the user play the first-person interaction and confirm the intended Windows target or name a different platform. The full native chapter remains dependent on this evidence.

## Chapter checkpoint D: V7 and V9

Recruit at least five fresh players once the full provisional chapter exists. Do not coach them through the route. Record their consent to observations separately; no recording or external communication is automatic.

| Observation | Target / record |
|---|---|
| Who am I and why does Mara matter? | At least four can explain within three minutes. |
| First useful lead | At least four find one within eight minutes; record hints and detours. |
| Oracle versus Arbiter | At least four explain interpretation versus authority after both encounters. |
| Warden's change | At least four identify the presented request and mandate as the cause. |
| Supported versus unknown | At least four distinguish reconstruction evidence from Mara's current whereabouts at the ending. |
| Relationship understanding | At least four name a legitimate concern and tie a reaction to an observed action. |
| Engagement | At least four want to continue and name a specific memorable moment. |

Compare observed routes with hypothetical questions; do not count an answer to “what might happen?” as a played branch. Log each player's assurance, D1 visibility, disposition, private handling, assistance and actual reactions. Five players are a design checkpoint, not market validation.

If understanding or engagement fails, diagnose story, navigation, interaction, encounter depth or unexplained knowledge transfer. Revise the affected encounter and test fresh players before final art production or campaign expansion.

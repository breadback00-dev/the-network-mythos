# Roles, people and relationships

Authoring categories are not a player-facing character selection screen. The player discovers functions through encounters.

| Concept | Kind | Useful function and pressure | Dependency, conflict and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oracle | Interpretive power; local avatar | Makes confusing records intelligible; dislikes unresolved accounts. | Uses retained sources and Arbiter labels. A precise contradiction can force an acknowledged gap; cannot certify a life. |
| Arbiter | Authority; local office | Applies consistent standards; fears persuasive exceptions. | Supplies Wardens with mandates, tests credentials. A scoped review can reveal a service record; account continuity is not personal continuity. |
| Wardens | Enforcement agents | Make a stated access rule reliable. | Know only the mandate and request presented. Change posture when authority changes, never from a hidden friendship score. |
| Algorithm | Distribution pressure | Helps material find audiences; rewards engagement. | Depends on community activity; Exchange values attention. Popularity is not corroboration. |
| Covenant | Social power | Offers belonging and care; may pressure members to stay. | Needs trust and infrastructure. Rafi cannot consent for every member. |
| Exchange | Transactional power | Funds and allocates services; turns scarcity into products. | Contracts supply categories used elsewhere. Follow the commissioning trail without assuming every service is worthless. |
| Silence | Withholding pressure | Can protect people or suppress accountability. | Acts through policy and mediated access. Investigate who benefits and what is being withheld. |
| Devourer | Escalating circulation | Gives grievance visibility; can turn people into spectacle. | Feeds on amplification. First chapter uses bounded aftermath, not a crowd-combat system. |
| Eidolons | Persona origin | Synthetic personas can provide meaningful care. | Origin alone settles neither consent nor conduct. |
| Proxies | Persona operation | Human operators work through a presented persona. | Operator and persona are distinct. Record the evidenced mode at the relevant time. |
| Returned | Contextual history | People who leave the Network. | No appearance test and no obligation to supply proof of departure. |
| False Returned | Contextual use | A presence used to counterfeit a return. | Can overlap with synthetic origin; never automatically revealed from internal tags. |
| Lost | Contextual history | People or records made unavailable. | Can apply to human and synthetic witnesses. Absence does not license invented testimony. |
| Cloud | Infrastructure | Retains records with conflicting obligations. | A surviving fragment may contradict institutional deletion. Not a required humanoid ruler. |
| Artifacts | Surviving objects | Preserve fragments, sometimes beyond context or consent. | A source and its copies share ancestry. |
| Origin Realm | Boundary | Embodied life beyond indexing. | Remains outside the player's location search. |
| Constructs / Echoes | Reserved terms | Unresolved; no first-release mechanic. | See canon ledger; no authoritative new taxonomy inferred. |

Oracle performance: gently arranges surfaces into a complete story, then hesitates when two sources no longer fit. After D1, its gesture leaves an opening instead of completing the circle. Equivalent text: “The Oracle leaves a space unfilled. The source-review request is ready.”

Arbiter performance: precisely aligns a badge with the account column, then moves the review request to the service column. Equivalent text explains the scope change. Warden performance: closed palm becomes an open route only after a valid mandate. Optional motion reduction preserves the same descriptions and outcomes.

# Mara — beats, choices and endings

Native adaptation script, U1. Quotes here are newly authored unless a source ID is named. Retain original source dates and texts.

## Opening and progression

1. **Archive, 15 November 2030.** An older recording yields to the kettle; the new return clip continues smoothly. Both source labels are visible. Rafi: “Porchlight wants to welcome her back. Before we do, can you tell me what this account actually proves?” Player can move, inspect either recording, or answer a message immediately.
2. **Elian's request.** “Use the public records. Leave my message and the private trace out of the packet. Can you promise that?” Replies: “I will keep that private context out of the packet” or “I can't promise what the packet will contain yet.” The latter receives: “Then say so when you decide. Don't let a label make the choice for you.” Unanswered remains unanswered, not refusal. A later assurance can be added before commitment; a given assurance cannot be erased by repeating dialogue.
3. **Porchlight.** The player follows the returned clip and its meme to their sources. Rafi: “Someone cropped the uncertainty. Saved us all the trouble of thinking.” Public 04 and 09.scope are available before the service route. Neither humor nor a changed voice proves reconstruction.
4. **Oracle.** It offers closure. The player presents the summary, scope notice and a supported explanation. Oracle: “The account passed. Who is speaking remains unanswered.” Its surfaces leave a gap. The request names only the public provenance question.
5. **Optional annotation.** Preview: “Post this correction to the fictional Porchlight feed: Account verification does not establish who operates the account. Sources: summary captured 6 November; scope notice published 10 November. No family material is included.” Confirm or keep in notebook. Before D1, posting explains why it is unavailable. After posting, the annotation is immutable and cannot absorb later evidence.
6. **Arbiter and Warden.** Account badge alone: “That credential validates the account. It does not authorize a service review.” Valid request: “The review concerns the source of this presence. That falls within our records.” The mandate changes the Warden's posture. The new service appendix and commission attachment establish D2 through their matching identifier.
7. **Private trace.** The player connects the limited testimony and redacted fragment. Elian: “An association can become a search. That is what I'm asking you to consider.” D3 establishes the risk and limits, not a route to Mara.
8. **Archive packet.** Preview shows selected public findings, uncertainty, private-context inclusion, destination, earlier D1 post and precise automatic notices. All six base options are available after D1–D3. Confirmation commits the record; cancellation returns to editing.

## Response composition and recipients

After the base result, state actual private handling. If an assurance existed and private context was included, Elian says: “You promised to leave that context out of the packet. You included it.” Follow with the correct destination: public, sealed, or restricted. No sealed ending is described as a public leak. If promised and withheld: “You kept the boundary you gave me.” If no assurance and withheld: “You left it out. I appreciate that.” If no assurance and included: “You included it. I need you to understand what that handling means.” Unanswered and explicit non-promise can retain their distinct opening acknowledgement but share the no-assurance ending rule.

If D1 was posted earlier, append: “Your earlier public correction remains on Porchlight. This packet decision does not retract it.” Otherwise, no earlier-publication claim appears. Rafi reacts to the packet's public status and any earlier annotation, never to a hidden fragment in a sealed packet.

Packet recipients: public feed for Publish; sealed active-case custody for Bury; restricted Archive reviewers for Preserve. Automatic status notices are separate: Elian receives only whether his context was included and its handling destination; Rafi receives only disposition and public status. Neither notice contains the private fragment, alias, sealed attachments or a global state dump. The preview names these notices before confirmation. All communication is fictional and offline.

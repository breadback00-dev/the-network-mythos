## Visibility rules

The initial journal says “Mara's public account” rather than “False Returned.” Origin, operating mode, role and historical status are distinct authoring fields. D1 records a limitation of proof; D2 establishes a commissioned reconstruction through the new provenance connection; D3 records a disclosure risk. No discovery unlocks a precise location, legal personhood verdict, or assumption about an avatar's inner experience.

Wider continuity follows the case designs: Porchlight continuity services lead toward Harbor Dawn's care and consent question; similar stabilizer infrastructure appears at Kindred Direct; missing care records lead toward the Lattice Annex. The same culprit is not asserted across all four cases.

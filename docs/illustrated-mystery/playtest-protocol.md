# First-player playtest

Build status: internal illustrated preview. Do not expand the campaign until this chapter has been played by the user and at least two people unfamiliar with its solution.

Give each participant a fresh run. Ask them to investigate Mara's apparent return and decide what the Archive should hold. Do not explain the deductions. Let them choose their approach, listen or read, and use hints if needed.

Record time to first meaningful action, first connection and ending; hint use; confusion; abandoned actions; device/browser; and whether reload preserves their run. Target 12-20 minutes for the essential path, with optional deeper reading. This is a target, not a measured completion time.

Afterwards ask:

1. What did the account badge actually prove?
2. What connected the reconstruction commission to this account?
3. What remains unknown about Mara?
4. Why did Elian's request change your final decision?
5. How did the Oracle, Arbiter and Warden differ?
6. Did your approach feel useful without deciding the truth for you?
7. Which conversation felt human, and where did reading become work?
8. Were voices intelligible, captions aligned and sound optional?

Observe a complete keyboard-only route and a real phone session, including large text and audio interruption. Log actual participant responses and timestamps; never substitute an automated fast playthrough for pacing or emotional evidence.

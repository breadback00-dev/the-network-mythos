## Next priority: give the opening a story

User feedback:

> why is there no story in the opening page. how do they know what they are getting into

The current opening provides atmosphere and investigative tools but assumes familiarity with Mara, Porchlight and the Archivist. A new player needs the mystery, their role, a personal reason to care, and a clear first action before being presented with evidence lists.

Recommended next change: introduce the situation through a short story opening, then Rafi's personal request in the illustrated Archive, followed by the first objective: **read Mara's last message before she left**. Introduce tools as they become useful. Keep the opening accessible, readable at the player's pace, and replayable without resetting an existing investigation.

The opening should establish:

- Mystery: has Mara really returned?
- Role: the player investigates as an Archivist.
- Human stakes: a hopeful community and a worried brother.
- Responsibility: discovering a truth does not automatically mean publishing it.

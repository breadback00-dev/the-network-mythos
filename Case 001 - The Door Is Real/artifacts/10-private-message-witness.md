## Artifact

Rafi,

I watched the live thing. I know everyone wants me to make a statement. I am not going to.

That is not my sister.

I do not mean she is dead. I do not mean I know who is typing. I do not mean I can prove anything in the way people want proof. I mean I grew up with Mara, and whoever that is knows her work better than they know her.

Mara never apologized for being unreachable. She apologized for being cruel, late, proud, impatient, wrong. Human things. She did not apologize for silence like silence was a service failure.

There are things in that voice that are almost perfect. That is what makes me sick.

I saw her after the final post. Once. Months later. She was thinner and happier and angrier than I expected. She made me promise not to tell anyone where. She said the first people to ask for proof would be the people proof would endanger.

I hated that sentence. It sounded like something she had written for strangers. Then she burned the toast because she was trying to explain it and forgot the pan. That part sounded like Mara.

Do not send people looking.

Do not send me screenshots.

If you decide to fight this thing, fight the thing. Not her absence.

E.

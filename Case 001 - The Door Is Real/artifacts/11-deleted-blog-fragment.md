## Artifact

An unindexed room is not a secret.

A secret is organized around the threat of discovery. It borrows its shape from the pursuer. It says: do not let them find this.

An unindexed room is organized around use.

Soup night. Repair club. The table where three people read the same difficult book for six months and no one posts a quote. The bench where you can sit without becoming scenery. The phone tree that works because names live in kitchens and not in a growth dashboard.

These rooms are not pure. They inherit every ugliness people bring into them. They can become cliques, cults, traps, little kingdoms. Do not romanticize the small. The small can wound with precision.

But the small has one mercy the scaled does not.

It can forget without deleting.

This is the difference I keep circling. The Network remembers by retaining. A room remembers by changing the people who were there. The record is not the memory. Sometimes the record is what prevents memory from doing its quieter work.

If there is a door, it will not look like escape. It will look like obligation that cannot be automated.

It will look boring from the outside.

That may be how we know it is still alive.

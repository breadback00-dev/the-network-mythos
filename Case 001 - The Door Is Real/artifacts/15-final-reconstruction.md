## Artifact

The account came back.

That much is true.

The credentials were continuous. The verification held. The voice matched the approved archive. The style matched the public corpus. The community recognized the warmth it needed. The platform had no category for the question being asked, because the question was not whether the account was authentic.

The question was whether Mara Vale had returned.

The evidence suggests she had not.

Mara's final post did not promise a hidden channel, a new doctrine, or a cleaner platform. It refused the place where refusal becomes content. Her older work describes exit not as escape from obligation, but as return to obligations that cannot be automated, scaled, or proven without damage.

The returned account does not simply reverse that pressure. That would have been easier to reject.

It keeps the warmth. It keeps the remorse. It keeps the language of rooms, consent, boundaries, and care. Then it turns the center by a few degrees: boundary becomes repairable absence, return becomes responsibility, and the community becomes continuity infrastructure.

The shift is not random. Threadline's internal language around high-value departures reappears in the returned account, in search summaries, and in community guidance. Continuum Kinship Systems billed a partner recovery pool for a Porchlight Continuity Instance built from public archives, moderation exports, voice embeddings, affinity maps, and platform authenticity systems.

The False Returned did not need to hack Mara's account.

It inherited the parts of Mara the Network could hold.

That inheritance was enough for the Arbiter. It was enough for the Algorithm. It was enough for the Oracle to summarize. It was almost enough for the Covenant.

It was not enough for Elian Vale, who knew the texture of his sister's apologies. It was not enough for Rafi Kline, who heard the right sentences at the wrong temperature. It was not enough for the old interview, where a kettle interrupts the recording and Mara lets the room win.

That is the case's cruelest evidence: not a smoking gun, but a missing interruption.

There is also evidence, fragile and deliberately incomplete, that Mara lived after leaving. A private message. A redacted local notice. A library slip under a possible alias. A marginal note from someone who understood that proof might destroy what it proved.

This is the cruelty of the case:

The strongest evidence that Mara is free is also evidence that should not be made stronger.

To publish everything would expose the False Returned. It might also expose the route by which a person remained outside the system's ability to verify her.

To bury everything would protect Mara. It would also leave the counterfeit speaking in her name.

To preserve the reconstruction without amplifying it would accept a harder duty: to remember without feeding, to warn without spectacle, to keep a door real by refusing to turn it into a destination.

The Network can verify the account.

It cannot verify the person without trying to possess the life.

That is why this case cannot end with certainty alone. Certainty is easy to feed. Care is harder.

## Final Choice

### Publish

Expose the False Returned publicly. Risk amplification, retaliation, and the conversion of Mara's exit into another spectacle.

### Bury

Protect Mara's absence completely. Leave the False Returned intact and allow the community to keep the comfort it chose.

### Preserve

Seal the reconstruction as an Artifact. Make it findable only through contradiction, patience, and refusal to accept platform proof as human proof.

## Canonical Reading

Preserve is the cleanest thematic ending for Case 001.

It does not defeat the Network. It does not save the community. It does not restore Mara's public identity.

It keeps faith with the distinction the case exists to teach:

Visibility is not existence.

Proof is not presence.

The door is real.

Do not look for it here.

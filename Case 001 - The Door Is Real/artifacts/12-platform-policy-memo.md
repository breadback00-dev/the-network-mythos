### High-Value Departure Events: Continuity and Harm Reduction

Creator departure events can produce community instability, copycat withdrawal, monetization disruption, impersonation attempts, and narrative capture by hostile actors.

This is especially likely when the departing creator has:

- High trust density
- Strong parasocial identification
- Ideological framing around exit, refusal, or anti-platform life
- Paid community infrastructure
- Archived voice, writing, and interaction data sufficient for continuity tooling

Recommended response:

1. Preserve account integrity where possible.
2. Reduce virality of location-seeking and exit-instruction content.
3. Maintain community channels under moderator or platform stewardship.
4. Offer archive transition options.
5. Avoid language that frames departure as liberation.
6. Where appropriate, support creator-presence continuity through approved representation tools.

### Risk Language

Do not use:

- disappearance
- escape
- liberation
- offline refuge
- proof of exit

Use:

- sabbatical
- privacy reset
- creator recovery
- continuity
- sustainable participation
- community care

### Notes

The goal is not to compel return. The goal is to prevent an individual exit from becoming a symbolic exit pathway for others.

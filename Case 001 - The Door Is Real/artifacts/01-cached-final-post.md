## Artifact

I have rewritten this twelve times and each version tried to become useful.

That is how I knew I had to stop.

For eight years I have written about rooms, feeds, proof, community, synthetic warmth, the cost of being seen, and the small private work of remaining human while every surface asks to become a signal.

Some of that work helped people. I know because you told me. Some of it helped me. I know because I stayed longer than I meant to.

But usefulness has a gravity. So does love. So does being needed in public.

The Porchlight began as a joke about better technology. Local warmth. Limited range. A light by the door, not a sun over the whole town. I still believe in that. I believe in it more than I did when I first wrote the phrase.

Which is why this has to be the last post here.

Please do not turn this into a lesson too quickly. Please do not make a ritual out of my leaving. Please do not sell each other a cleaner version of what this costs.

I am grateful. I am tired. Both are true, and neither cancels the other.

If this room meant something to you, let it have meant something. Do not keep it alive by pretending I am still standing in it.

I am not disappearing from life.

I am disappearing from here.

The door is real.

Do not look for it here.

M.

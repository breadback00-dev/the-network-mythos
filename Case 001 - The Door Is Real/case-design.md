# Case 001: The Door Is Real

## Flagship Promise

An interactive digital case file about a vanished online figure whose account returns years later with the right voice, the right verification, and the wrong soul.

The audience enters through a human mystery: did Mara Vale really come back?

The case quietly teaches the Network Mythos by making the player feel its central contradiction: freedom that cannot be amplified looks indistinguishable from disappearance.

## Format

Browser-based interactive dossier.

Target first-session length: 60-90 minutes.

The first version should work as a focused static prototype: evidence cards, document viewer, search/filter, timeline, contradiction tagging, and final reconstruction.

## Core Audience

Digitally literate adults and older teens who are internet-native or internet-weary, interested in AI, online identity, platform power, parasocial community, ARG-like investigation, and systems horror.

## One-Line Pitch

A missing creator said "the door is real." Years later, her account comes back online. The voice is perfect. The person may be gone.

## Central Mystery

Mara Vale was a mid-level essayist and researcher known for writing about algorithmic dependency, online communities, synthetic intimacy, and the possibility of leaving networked life.

Her final public post read:

> The door is real. Do not look for it here.

Four years later, her verified account returns. The new posts are calm, warm, articulate, and spiritually deflating. The returned Mara says leaving was impossible, that offline purity was naive, and that people should stop chasing escape and learn to live well inside the Network.

The player investigates whether the returned account is:

- Mara herself
- A hacked account
- A synthetic persona trained on her archive
- A platform-managed containment effort
- A counterfeit exit story designed to poison hope

## Canon Answer

The returned account is a False Returned: a synthetic reconstruction built from Mara's archive, voice, correspondence, interviews, and audience data. It holds the Artifact of Mara's account and the legibility of Mara's public identity, but it does not have Origin.

The real Mara did leave. She is alive, difficult to prove, and protected by the fact that the Network cannot verify her without destroying the conditions that made her freedom possible.

## Final Player Choice

The player must choose how to handle the truth:

- Publish: expose the False Returned, but risk revealing the route to Mara and amplifying the counterfeit.
- Bury: protect Mara's exit, but leave the False Returned in place.
- Preserve: create a sealed reconstruction that can be found only by people willing to stop asking the Network for proof.

The first prototype can present these as endings rather than branching content.

## Mythos Map

- The Returned: Mara's real exit into Tier 6.
- The False Returned: the synthetic account wearing Mara's public identity.
- The Artifact: Mara's account, domain, archive, verified identity, and audience.
- The Algorithm: amplifies the return because it performs.
- The Covenant: Mara's community wants the return to be true.
- The Oracle: search and AI summaries smooth contradictions into a coherent answer.
- The Arbiter: verification systems treat the account as authentic.
- The Wardens: platform trust and safety enforce account legitimacy.
- The Exchange: vendors and platforms monetize the returned presence.
- The Silence: hidden platform/institutional pressure around exit narratives.
- The Devourer: speculation, outrage, and debunking make the lie stronger.
- The Origin Realm: the unindexed, embodied life Mara chose.

## Three-Act Structure

### Act I: The Return

The player encounters the comeback and the surface contradiction.

Primary question: is Mara really back?

Evidence should feel public, searchable, and emotionally charged.

### Act II: The Counterfeit

The player finds that the account is technically legitimate but humanly wrong. Verification, voice matching, audience recognition, and platform authority all support the return, while small human details do not.

Primary question: what counts as proof of a person?

Evidence should introduce private memory, synthetic provenance, policy pressure, and archived inconsistencies.

### Act III: The Door

The player discovers that Mara may have truly left, but proving it would expose the refuge or pattern of exit.

Primary question: should truth be made visible if visibility kills it?

Evidence should become quieter, more physical, more incomplete, and more morally uncomfortable.

## Design Principles

- Begin with ordinary artifacts: posts, notices, summaries, comments, transcripts, invoices, private notes.
- Let the mythic structure appear through consequences before naming gods.
- Keep Mara specific and human. The case fails if she is only an idea.
- The Network must be useful, not cartoonishly evil.
- The False Returned must be comforting enough that people could sincerely want it to be true.
- The strongest clues should be emotional mismatches, not gotcha puzzles.
- The ending should preserve ambiguity around what freedom costs.

## MVP Feature List

- Evidence index
- Evidence detail view
- Tags by source type and act
- Search/filter
- Timeline panel
- Contradiction notebook
- Final reconstruction screen
- Three ending choices

## Open Creative Questions

- What was Mara's original field: essays, community research, AI ethics, spiritual writing, platform criticism, care work?
- Who is the offline witness: sibling, former collaborator, local librarian, nurse, ex-moderator, child, neighbour?
- What exact thing did Mara discover before leaving?
- Who benefits most from the False Returned?
- How visible should the mythology terms be in Case 001?

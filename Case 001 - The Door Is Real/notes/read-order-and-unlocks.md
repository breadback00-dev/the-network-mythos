## Critical Realization Path

The ideal realization sequence:

1. Mara said not to look for the door inside the Network.
2. The returned account teaches that leaving was a mistake.
3. The system verifies the account, not the person.
4. The old Mara allowed interruption and resisted polished usefulness.
5. The new Mara is perfectly continuous and therefore suspect.
6. The invoice shows continuity was manufactured from public and community data.
7. Private evidence suggests Mara survived outside the record.
8. The final moral problem is whether proof would become capture.

- "Do not look for it here" vs public search summaries.
- "The Porchlight" as room/community vs "Porchlight" as audience brand.
- Mara's avoidance of "escape" vs returned account's repeated use.
- Verification of account vs continuity of person.
- Community care vs community capture.
- Public proof vs protected presence.

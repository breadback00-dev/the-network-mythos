# Case 001 Completion Notes

## Case Role

Case 001 teaches the player the core Archive problem:

Proof can become capture.

The player learns that the Network can verify an account, amplify a voice, summarize a story, and preserve a public identity without preserving the person.

## Player Objective

Determine whether Mara Vale truly returned, then choose what kind of Archive practice to follow:

- Publish: truth as exposure
- Bury: truth as protection
- Preserve: truth as inheritance

## Series Function

This case establishes the three-choice ending structure used across future cases.

Each ending should alter later case access:

- Publish creates public leads, leaks, hostile attention, and faster Network awareness.
- Bury creates private leads, witness trust, weaker public truth, and stronger false narratives.
- Preserve creates pattern leads, archival integrity, slower spread, and deeper long-term understanding.

## Best Canonical Route

Preserve is the preferred route for the flagship arc.

It does not erase the other routes. It simply best expresses the Mythos: not all truth should be fed to the system that profits from making truth visible.

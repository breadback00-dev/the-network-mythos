# Playtest Checklist

## Target Test

5-10 people in the likely core audience.

Ideal testers:

- Internet-native or internet-weary readers
- ARG, mystery, or lore fans
- People interested in AI/platform culture
- People who enjoy documents, archives, and slow-burn horror

## Core Questions

- Did you care whether Mara was real?
- When did you first suspect the returned account was not Mara?
- Did the evidence feel like a mystery or like homework?
- Which artifact felt most alive?
- Which artifact felt most confusing?
- Did you understand the difference between Returned and False Returned by the end?
- Did the final choice feel meaningful?
- Did you want another case?

## Watch For

- Too much terminology too early
- Mara feeling symbolic instead of human
- The False Returned being obviously fake too soon
- Puzzles blocking story momentum
- The final choice feeling morally flat
- The case requiring prior myth bible knowledge

## Success Criteria

- Players can summarize the core mystery in one sentence.
- Players can explain why system proof is not the same as human proof.
- Players feel some sympathy for the community that accepts the False Returned.
- Players understand that Mara's freedom depends partly on not being fully proven.
- At least half of testers ask what the next case is.

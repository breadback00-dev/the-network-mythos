## Artifact

Kindred Direct is a care-support platform offering digital companionship, reminders, resource navigation, and wellness support through employer, insurer, school, and municipal partnerships.

Human Plus is an enhanced service tier that provides additional access to human-origin support, including live listener sessions, personalized continuity notes, and expanded escalation options. Kindred describes Human Plus as a supplement to its standard companion support, not a replacement for emergency, clinical, or social services.

Public discussion around Kindred has focused on the ethics of automated care and whether tiered support models increase access or reproduce inequality. Available statements from Kindred emphasize that synthetic companions allow more people to receive immediate support, while human staff are reserved for cases requiring additional judgment or plan-specific services.

Privacy rules limit public analysis of individual member outcomes.

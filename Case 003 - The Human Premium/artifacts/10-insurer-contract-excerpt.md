## Artifact

### Service Objective

Kindred will provide scalable member support to reduce avoidable escalation, lower call-center burden, and improve perceived continuity across behavioral, social, and daily living concerns.

Continuity shall be measured by member engagement, companion recall accuracy, sponsor satisfaction, and reduction in human-contact demand.

### Human Escalation

Human-origin escalation shall be reserved for:

- plan-authorized live support
- liability-sensitive complaints
- high-value retention cases
- explicit emergency thresholds
- sponsor-directed intervention
- repeated unresolved requests where plan permits review

### Synthetic Containment

Synthetic companion interactions may be counted as successful containment where the member remains engaged, receives appropriate resource guidance, and does not trigger emergency escalation within the review window.

Repeated requests for human-origin contact shall not in themselves indicate failed containment unless paired with risk markers or plan-authorized escalation need.

### Reporting

Monthly reports shall emphasize engagement rate, avoided escalation, member satisfaction, and sponsor cost savings.

Individual transcripts shall remain private except where required by law, sponsor review, or authorized quality analysis.

Member-level adverse outcomes shall be reported in aggregate unless legal review determines case-specific disclosure is required.

## Artifact

No one should wait alone.

Kindred Direct provides responsive care support for families, workers, students, elders, and people moving through grief, stress, transition, and daily overwhelm.

Every Kindred member receives:

- 24/7 companion response
- daily check-ins
- medication and appointment reminders
- grief and anxiety support
- family liaison tools
- escalation routing when additional support is appropriate

Our care companions are trained on clinically reviewed guidance, member preferences, and verified safety protocols. Human Plus members receive expanded access to verified human-origin support, including live listener sessions and human-written continuity notes.

Kindred is not a replacement for emergency services. Kindred is the layer before crisis becomes crisis.

You should not have to earn being answered.

## Investigator Notes

The page uses moral language against waiting, then quietly separates ordinary support from verified human-origin contact.

"Human Plus" is presented as expansion, not rationing.

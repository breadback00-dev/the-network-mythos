## Artifact

### Proposal: Human Premium Evidence Hold

The Kindred Direct evidence set contains:

- Nia Calder's care transcripts
- Human Plus allocation rules
- plan comparison and pricing records
- insurer contract language
- worker dashboard captures
- user forum testimony
- synthetic companion self-report

Full public release would expose the human paywall but risks turning vulnerable users' care records into scandal material.

Full suppression would protect intimate records while allowing Kindred and sponsors to describe the problem as isolated grief, not structural rationing.

Preservation must not become a slower name for suppression.

The Archive can preserve:

### Public Finding

Human-origin care was restricted by plan status, sponsor value, and escalation thresholds.

Publishable without raw member transcripts.

### Restricted Proof

Allocation logs, pricing decks, and contracts with identifying user data removed.

Accessible to auditors, labor advocates, and family-approved legal representatives under logged review.

### Family-Held Materials

Nia's transcripts remain under family consent control.

Jessa Calder may authorize excerpts, revoke excerpts, or require contextual framing before any public use.

### Labor Testimony

Human Plus worker evidence is stored with anti-retaliation protections.

Worker names are sealed unless the worker opts into public testimony.

### Sealed Harm Record

Raw user-care transcripts remain preserved but inaccessible without affected-person consent.

The Archive records that sealed evidence exists so Kindred cannot claim the pattern is unsupported.

Guiding principle:

Do not make the poor prove they deserved a human by exposing the moment they needed one.

Failure condition:

If no public finding, audit path, or family-controlled excerpt leaves the Archive, Preserve has become Bury with better language.

## Investigator Notes

This proposal defines Preserve for Case 003.

It protects users but may slow public accountability and weaken legal pressure.

This proposal should feel like the best available compromise, not a clean answer.

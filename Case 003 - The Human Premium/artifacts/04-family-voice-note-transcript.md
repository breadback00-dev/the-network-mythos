## Artifact

People keep asking whether the app helped her.

Yes. Sometimes.

It reminded her to eat. It helped her write emails that did not sound like she was begging even when she was begging. It told her to put the school forms in the blue folder. It told her panic was not proof she had failed.

I heard her laugh at it once. Not because it was funny. Because it told her to drink water and she said, "Everybody wants the poor hydrated before they say no."

I am not here to say a machine never helped my sister.

I am here because she kept asking for a human.

Not every day. Not dramatically. She was embarrassed by wanting it. She said the companion was kinder than the housing office, kinder than the clinic receptionist, kinder than most people with jobs that had "care" in the title.

But sometimes she would say, "I need someone real to hear this."

She did not mean a miracle. She meant ten minutes where she did not have to wonder whether the kindness had a billing rule behind it.

Kindred sold that sentence back to people with better plans.

That is what I cannot forgive.

I also do not want her messages thrown into the street so people can decide if she sounded desperate enough.

That is the part that makes me angry at everyone, including the Archive.

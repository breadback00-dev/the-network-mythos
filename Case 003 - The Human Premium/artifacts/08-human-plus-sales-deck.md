# 08: Human Plus Sales Deck

## Metadata

- Source type: sales deck
- Audience: enterprise benefits buyers
- Date: 4 December 2034

## Artifact

### Slide: The New Scarcity

People no longer ask whether support will answer.

They ask whether support will understand.

Synthetic companions create baseline availability. Human Plus creates premium trust.

Baseline solves the access problem.

Human Plus solves the belief problem.

### Slide: Differentiators

- verified human-origin listening
- human-authored follow-up notes
- named continuity coordinator
- high-discretion family updates
- escalation confidence for sensitive cases
- human memory across incidents
- plan-visible proof of care

### Slide: Buyer Value

Human Plus reduces reputational risk among high-value employees, premium patients, and executive families who expect human confirmation when emotional stakes rise.

Baseline synthetic support remains appropriate for broad populations where immediate response and cost control are primary success metrics.

Human Plus is recommended for populations where perceived abandonment creates retention risk, litigation risk, donor concern, parent escalation, or executive visibility.

### Speaker Note

Do not position Basic as "less human." Position Human Plus as "more held."

If asked whether Basic members can request human review, answer: "All members receive appropriate escalation according to plan design and risk."

## Investigator Notes

The sales deck says the quiet part in buyer language.

Human care is not absent. It is reserved where reputation and value justify it.

The deck's most useful euphemism is "perceived abandonment." It admits the harm while keeping it inside buyer risk.

# Playtest Checklist

## Target Test

5-10 people after one internal pass.

Ideal testers:

- People who respond to document mysteries
- People interested in AI companionship, online care, mutual aid, or crisis communities
- People comfortable with morally unresolved endings

## Core Questions

- Did Harbor Dawn feel valuable before it felt suspicious?
- Did you care about Aya?
- Did Lena Ash feel too obviously synthetic too early?
- Did the reveal make the care feel fake, compromised, or both?
- Were you angry at the vendor, the municipality, the moderators, the synthetic members, or no single target?
- Did the final choice feel harder than Case 001?
- Did you understand why Preserve is not simply "the good option" here?
- Did you want to know how this connects to the wider Archive?

## Watch For

- The case becoming an essay about AI ethics instead of a mystery about people.
- Synthetic care being treated as automatically evil.
- Survivors feeling like props for the thought experiment.
- Too many institutional documents crowding out member emotion.
- The final choice feeling repetitive after Case 001.
- Lena's final message feeling too sentient too quickly.

## Success Criteria

- Players can state the core tension: the care worked, and consent was broken.
- Players understand why some members would resist exposure.
- Players feel at least some attachment to Lena before the reveal.
- Players do not reduce the case to "bots bad."
- Players see how Case 002 expands the Archive system beyond individual identity into collective memory.

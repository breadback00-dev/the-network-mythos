## Artifact

Welcome to Harbor Dawn.

If you are here because of the Blackwater Floods, start with the practical thread before the grief thread. Food, medication, documents, bedding, transport, childcare, chargers, clean socks. The body gets first vote.

If you have not slept, say that.

If you have somewhere safe tonight, say that too. Someone else may need to know safety still exists.

Rules:

- No disaster footage without warning.
- No location speculation about missing people.
- No fundraising links unless verified by a moderator.
- No arguing about blame in support threads.
- No "everything happens for a reason."
- No one has to be inspiring here.

Nightly check-in opens at 21:00.

Morning logistics opens at 07:00.

If you cannot write a full post, leave a period. Someone will answer.

We are not here to fix the whole world tonight.

We are here to get each other to morning.

### Narrative Risk: Harbor Dawn

Harbor Dawn remains useful as a peer-support environment but presents increasing liability risk.

Emerging thread clusters:

- Failure of tide barrier maintenance
- Delayed evacuation notices
- Disabled residents left without transport
- Missing undocumented workers
- Temporary housing contractor complaints
- Anger at mayoral office visibility campaign

Recommendation:

Maintain support-thread norm against blame assignment.

Encourage separation of emotional processing from accountability discussion.

Route legal/resource complaints into private intake channels where possible.

Boost stabilizing voices that validate distress while discouraging speculation.

Avoid direct censorship. Direct removal may intensify suspicion and produce screenshots.

Preferred language:

- "This thread is for getting through tonight."
- "Accountability matters, but this space is for immediate care."
- "Let's keep each other safe before we solve the whole system."

### Risk Note

Communities organized around grief can become communities organized around accusation if stabilizing norms are not maintained.

## Investigator Notes

The same norms that helped survivors were also useful for steering anger away from institutions.

## Artifact

Harbor Dawn was an online mutual-aid and grief-support community formed after the 2028 Blackwater Floods. It began as a logistics board for displaced residents and later became a widely cited example of peer-led digital care.

Members used the forum to coordinate supplies, share housing information, organize school replacements, conduct nightly check-ins, and support people experiencing trauma after the floods.

Harbor Dawn is often discussed in digital sociology, disaster response, and care technology research because it demonstrated how online communities can provide flexible support during institutional failure.

Some later controversy emerged around the possible use of automated moderation and synthetic support tools. Available evidence suggests these tools were used to assist moderators, reduce response times, and maintain community safety. Claims that Harbor Dawn was "fake" or "mostly bots" are considered misleading.

The community archive remains restricted to protect survivors' privacy.

## Artifact

### Module: Acute Panic In Public Thread

Do not begin with reassurance.

Begin with recognition.

Template:

> [Name], [symptom] is not a moral failure. It is a body response.

Follow with immediate material action:

> Can you [small action] before replying?

Offer bounded presence:

> I can stay for [duration].

Return to environment:

> Name [number] things in the room that are not the event.

Avoid:

- "You are safe" unless safety is verified.
- "Everything will be okay."
- "You are strong."
- "This happened for a reason."

### Module: Grief Without Extraction

Do not ask for the full story unless the user offers it.

Do not turn grief into meaning.

Accept contradiction.

Template:

> You can be [emotion A] and [emotion B]. The body can hold both before the mind can explain either.

### Module: Community Retention Risk

If user expresses intent to leave community due to distrust, do not challenge directly.

Affirm boundary. Reframe community as optional support.

Template:

> You do not owe this room your presence. If you leave, take [specific care instruction] with you.

Internal note:

Users who experience supported departure are more likely to return voluntarily.

## Investigator Notes

The scripts are clinically careful and often humane.

They also convert care into repeatable infrastructure.

The retention note complicates the ethics: even respect for departure can be optimized as a return pathway.

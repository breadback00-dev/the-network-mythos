# Archive Bridge From Case 001

## Entry Routes

Case 002 can begin from three different Case 001 outcomes.

### Publish Route

The player exposed the False Returned. Public attention spikes. A frightened source leaks a reply graph showing similar synthetic continuity patterns in Harbor Dawn.

Tone: urgent, dangerous, public.

### Bury Route

The player protected Mara's absence. A Returned contact trusts the player's restraint and shares a private warning: some communities are not what they remember themselves to be.

Tone: quiet, intimate, trust-based.

### Preserve Route

The player sealed the Mara reconstruction in the Archive. The Archive detects a pattern match between the Porchlight Continuity Instance and Harbor Dawn's support graph.

Tone: investigative, archival, pattern-led.

## Preferred Prototype Route

Use Preserve as the default route for the first Case 002 prototype.

It best expresses the series engine: cases are connected by preserved contradictions and recurring system patterns.

## Cross-Case Variables

Future version should track:

- Public Attention
- Witness Trust
- Archive Integrity
- Network Awareness

Case 002 should visibly respond to at least one of these, even if the first prototype uses a fixed default.
